01 / OUR FIRST MISSION

The Dennis Initiative

ACTIVE RESEARCH FOCUS

Understanding pancreatic cancer treatment resistance.

Why do some cancers resist treatment? EUREKA’s first mission brings together scientific literature to help frame better questions about the mechanisms behind resistance.

The mission honours Dennis. Its pancreatic cancer focus is a research choice; we do not make a claim about his original diagnosis.

FROM QUESTIONS TO INVESTIGATION

  1. Monitor emerging researchWorking prototype: retrieve publications from Europe PMC and PubMed.
  2. Explore connectionsWorking prototype: flag shared scientific terminology for private literature review. Whether a connection is unexplored is unassessed.
  3. Generate new hypothesesPlanned: propose testable scientific questions. Originality requires a prior-art search and expert assessment.
  4. Evaluate against evidence and dataPlanned: assess supporting and conflicting evidence, then integrate suitable public biological datasets.
  5. Request qualified reviewPrivate review candidates are supported. Verified researcher review and any discovery alarm are future work.

A journey, one careful step at a time.

A Python literature engine, private evidence vault, source links, and a research dashboard. The private GitHub Actions workflow has completed successful cloud runs. Its six-hour schedule is configured independently of ChatGPT.

04 / HOW WE WORK

Open about the journey.
Careful with the research.

Our purpose is to support scientific investigation, not to promise a cure. A hypothesis is a question to test. A paper is a source to evaluate. Neither alone establishes a treatment.

Novel hypotheses, sensitive evidence, unpublished findings, and the research engine remain private. Public updates share only aggregate activity and development milestones.

Any promising idea needs qualified scientific review. Researcher outreach will require owner approval before sending. The discovery alarm remains disabled.
