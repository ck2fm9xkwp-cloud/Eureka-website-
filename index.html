01 / OUR MISSION

The Dennis Initiative

MISSION 001

Searching for answers in cancer research.

EUREKA began with a personal loss and a question: what more can we learn from science to help fight cancer?

Our mission is to explore the research that exists today, investigate new possibilities and help identify questions worth pursuing in the search for better treatments.

OUR APPROACH

  1. Explore the scienceCollect and organize existing and emerging scientific publications.
  2. Connect ideasLook for relationships between studies that may be worth investigating.
  3. Ask new questionsWork toward generating and evaluating original scientific hypotheses.
  4. Build on evidenceDevelop methods to evaluate promising ideas and seek expert review.

04 / OUR COMMITMENT

Curiosity.
Evidence. Purpose.

We share our journey through research milestones and verified progress. Unpublished findings remain confidential until they are ready for appropriate scientific review.

Every potential discovery begins as a question. We believe in careful investigation, honest reporting and the importance of qualified scientific review.

EUREKA supports research toward better cancer treatments. It does not offer medical advice or claim a proven cure.
